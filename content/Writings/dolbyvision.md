## Video

1. 编码格式
   1. H.264/AVC(Advanced Video Coding)
   1. H.265/HEVC(High Efficiency Video Coding)
      - HEVC 的效率大约是 AVC 的两倍（低于H.264 30%~50%的比特率，降低内存，节省硬件和网络传输成本）
      - HEVC 支持 4K 和高动态范围(HDR, Dolby)

## 优劣对比

主流HDR格式: HDR10, HDR10+, Dloby Vision, HLG(旨在用于直播, 将SDR与HDR混合, 动态二选一)

| Feature                 | HDR10     | HDR10+    | DolbyVision |
| ----------------------- | --------- | --------- | ----------- |
| Bit Depth               | Good      | Great     | Great       |
| Peak Brightness Minimum | Good      | Good      | Great       |
| Peak Brightness Maximum | Excellent | Excellent | Excellent   |
| Tone Mapping            | Good      | Better    | Best        |
| Metadata                | Static    | Dynamic   | Dynamic     |
| TV Support              | Amazing   | Good      | Great       |
| Content Availability    | Beat      | Great     | Excellent   |
